PK PageKind Scanner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-03

PageKind Scanner does not collect, transmit, or share any user data.

PageKind Scanner is a native iOS application that scans QR codes and captures photos using your iPhone camera. Privacy is a core design principle: the app communicates with no servers and keeps everything on your device.

Data We Collect

None. PageKind Scanner does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Camera

The app requests access to your device camera to scan QR codes and capture still photos. Video frames are processed entirely on-device using Apple's AVFoundation and Vision frameworks. No images or video are sent anywhere.

Photos Library

When you tap Save after capturing a photo, the app writes that image to your iOS Photos library using Apple's PHPhotoLibrary API. PageKind Scanner requests add-only access — it can save new photos but cannot read your existing library. The image you save is then governed by iOS's normal Photos management.

Scan History

When you scan a QR code, the decoded URL is saved to a local list stored in your app's private UserDefaults on your device. This list:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Holds a maximum of 20 entries, oldest removed first
  • Can be cleared at any time via the History tab

Third-Party Services

PageKind Scanner uses no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising networks, or crash-reporting tools. The binary contains only Apple system frameworks.
