Support

PageKind Scanner is a simple, private QR code scanner and camera app for iPhone. There's no account, no backend, and nothing to sign up for — so most things just work.

Common Questions

The camera won't open.

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and make sure PageKind Scanner is enabled. If the option doesn't appear, try force-quitting and reopening the app.

The QR scan isn't triggering.

Make sure the QR code fills most of the viewfinder and is well-lit — use the torch button (⚡) in the top-left corner if you're in a dim environment. Damaged or very small codes may not scan reliably.

I can't save a photo to my library.

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos and make sure PageKind Scanner is set to Add Photos Only or Full Access. The app requests add-only access and cannot read your existing library.

How do I clear my scan history?

Open the History tab and tap Edit to remove individual entries, or tap Clear All to remove everything at once.

The photo is saving sideways.

Make sure you hold your iPhone in portrait orientation when capturing. The app is portrait-only and records orientation in the image's EXIF data — if a photo appears rotated in another app, that app may not be respecting the EXIF orientation field.

Does the app send my scans or photos anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.
